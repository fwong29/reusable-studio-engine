Cambiode Raíz
Type the Spanish stem-changers — and get the -ar / -er / -ir ending right.

How to play

1You get an infinitive and a subject pronoun. Type the full conjugated form — verb only, no pronoun.
2Every answer is two decisions: does the stem change here? and which ending does this verb take? You're scored on both.
3Miss it and the game shows you the form split into stem + ending and tells you which half you got wrong.
4Beat the clock. Streaks multiply your points. At the end you get a breakdown of what to review.

-ar endings

  • o · as · a
  • amos · áis · an

-er endings

  • o · es · e
  • emos · éis · en

-ir endings

  • o · es · e
  • imos · ís · en

-er and -ir only split apart in nosotros and vosotros. That's where most points get lost.
